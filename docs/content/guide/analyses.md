An analysis is a written account of an investigation: what you looked at,
what you found, what it means. It lives in the investigation, next to the
sketches, and every member who can read the investigation can read it.

## Writing an analysis

On the investigation page, **+ New analysis** creates one and opens it on
its own page. Click the title to rename it.

The editor is a rich text editor with a toolbar: bold, italic,
strikethrough, inline code, three heading levels, bullet and numbered
lists, quotes, code blocks, a divider, links, and undo and redo.

Your work saves itself a second after you stop typing. The header says
**Saving...** and then **Saved** with the time. **Cmd/Ctrl+S** saves at
once.

Editors and above can write. Deleting an analysis takes an admin or the
owner.

## Mentioning entities

Type `@` and start typing a name. A list of the entities of the
investigation's sketches opens, matched by a piece of their label. Pick
one with the arrows and Enter (or Tab), or a click. It becomes a chip in
the text.

Click a chip to open its sketch with that entity in focus. The **@**
button in the toolbar starts a mention too.

A mention points to the entity itself. Moving the investigation to
another install as one export file keeps every mention pointing to the
right entity.

## Notes beside the graph

In a sketch, **Notes** in the header (or **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+L**) opens the
investigation's analyses in the side panel, so you can write while you
look at the graph.

- The select at the top switches between the analyses, **New** starts
  another, and the arrow opens the current one on its own page.
- Typing `@` there offers the entities of this sketch. Clicking a chip
  selects the entity on the canvas, or opens its sketch when it lives in
  another one.
- It saves itself like the full page.

For a short note about one entity, use the **Notes** box in that entity's
panel instead (see [Entities](/guide/entities/#notes)).
